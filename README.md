# Care Cost Planner

An assisted-living **cost & runway comparison tool** for communities in the
Franklin, TN area.

Configure two setups side by side — community, room, number of residents, and
care levels — and instantly see each option's **total annual cost** and **how
many years your savings will last**, calculated both **with** and **without**
the **VA Aid & Attendance** benefit.

## Usage

It's a single, self-contained `index.html` file with no build step and no
dependencies. Open it in any modern browser, or visit the published site.

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

All figures are transcribed from each community's printed rate sheet and are
**planning estimates only** — not financial, tax, or legal advice. Rates change;
confirm current pricing and benefit eligibility directly with each community and
the VA before making any decisions.
